Discover the 2025 Nissan Rogue

Get ready to elevate your driving experience with the remarkable 2025 Nissan Rogue, now available at New City Nissan in Honolulu. This compact SUV is engineered to exceed your expectations with its stunning design, advanced technology, and exceptional performance. Whether you're navigating the bustling streets of Waipahu, commuting to Schofield Barracks, or exploring the scenic routes of Kapolei, the Nissan Rogue is your ideal companion.

Performance

The 2025 Nissan Rogue is powered by a 1.5-liter turbocharged engine, which can deliver an impressive 201 horsepower. This innovative engine utilizes direct injection and variable compression technology to optimize performance and efficiency. With its responsive acceleration and smooth handling, the Nissan Rogue makes every drive a thrilling experience. Whether you opt for the front-wheel drive or the intelligent all-wheel drive system, you can expect confident and stable performance in various driving conditions.

Exterior

The exterior of the Nissan Rogue is a masterpiece of modern design. Its sleek and aerodynamic silhouette is enhanced by bold lines and sculpted contours, creating a commanding presence on the road. The distinctive grille, flanked by LED headlights, adds a touch of sophistication to the front fascia. The available 19-inch aluminum alloy wheels not only enhance the aesthetic appeal but also provide superb stability and grip. With a range of captivating color options, you can personalize your Nissan Rogue to reflect your unique style.

Interior

Step into the Nissan Rogue and be greeted by a roomy and meticulously crafted interior. The cabin is crafted with comfort and convenience in mind, offering ample legroom and headroom for both front and rear passengers. The available quilted semi-aniline leather-appointed seats provide a feeling of luxury, while the dual-zone automatic temperature control ensures a pleasant atmosphere for everyone on board.

Safety

The 2025 Nissan Rogue comes equipped with a comprehensive suite of advanced safety features designed to provide peace of mind on every journey. Some of the standout safety technologies include:

  • Automatic Emergency Braking with Pedestrian Detection
  • Blind Spot Warning
  • Rear Cross Traffic Alert
  • Lane Departure Warning
  • High Beam Assist
  • Rear Automatic Braking

These top-notch systems all work together to monitor your surroundings, alert you to possible hazards, and intervene when necessary to help avoid collisions. Whether you're navigating the busy streets of Waipahu or cruising along the highways near Schofield Barracks, the advanced safety features of the Nissan Rogue provide another layer of protection for you and your loved ones.

Trim Levels

The new Nissan Rogue offers a variety of trims to suit different preferences and lifestyles. Each trim builds upon the previous one, adding more advanced features and premium amenities. The available trim levels include:

  • Nissan Rogue S
  • Nissan Rogue SV
  • Nissan Rogue SL
  • Nissan Rogue Platinum
  • Nissan Rogue Rock Creek

From the well-equipped S trim to the luxurious Platinum and the rugged Rock Creek edition, there's a Nissan Rogue that perfectly matches your needs and desires. Whether you prioritize advanced safety features, premium audio systems, or all-terrain capabilities, the Nissan Rogue lineup has you covered. Visit New City Nissan in Honolulu to explore the different trim options and find the one that speaks to you.

Technology

The 2025 Nissan Rogue is packed with advanced technology features that keep you informed and entertained. The infotainment system offers seamless integration with your smartphone through wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™. Access your favorite apps, music, and navigation with just a few taps.
